# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior:
- Welcoming language
- Respectful discussion
- Constructive feedback
- Community focus
- Empathy and kindness

Unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment
- Offensive comments
- Intimidation
- Discrimination
- Unwelcome advances

## Enforcement

Instances may be reported to conduct@example.com.
